Relay frontend: three design options

Static prototypes that share the same mock data and behaviour: login, dashboard, data table with AND/OR search, highlight, filters and Add row, and the team page. They run in English and Spanish (toggle in each). A new event arrives on the dashboard every 9 seconds to show the polling effect. Open them in a browser, no build needed.

A. Ledger

The product looks like the thing your team already uses: a spreadsheet.

Type
Schibsted Grotesk
Shape
Square-ish, 1px grid lines, dense
Layout
Formula bar summarises today. Sheet tabs along the bottom are the navigation. The dashboard feed is a real grid with sheet row numbers.
Motion
Minimal and quick (120 to 160 ms). New events flash like an edited cell. Fade dialog.
Best for
Power users who scan a lot of rows. Fewest surprises.
Trade-off
Least friendly look for non-technical members. Bottom tabs are unusual on the web.
OpenDataTeamLogin

B. Dispatch

Relay as a live feed: the story of what just changed comes first.

Type
Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk
Shape
Soft, large radii on panels, smaller on controls
Layout
Left sidebar. The headline is today's count with a proportional bar. A timeline is the main column.
Motion
New events slide into the timeline. A progress line counts down to the next poll. Page changes cross-fade with a hint of blur. One staggered entrance on first load.
Best for
A mixed team where most people look, a few edit. Friendliest.
Trade-off
Takes more vertical space. The timeline holds less per screen than a table.
OpenDataTeamLogin

C. Observatory

Activity over time is the hero: the 30-day chart comes first, the feed second.

Type
Sora + Figtree
Shape
Rounded, quiet panels, pill navigation
Layout
Top bar with centered tabs. A tall stacked chart; legend buttons turn each action on or off. Hover a bar for the breakdown.
Motion
Bars grow once on first load. Add row opens as a side drawer. Toasts drop from the top.
Best for
Admins who want to see activity patterns. Works well on large screens and at night.
Trade-off
Dark only (a light theme needs extra work). Chart-first means the feed sits below the fold.
OpenDataTeamLogin
These are visual studies, not the app. In the real project the same screens use shadcn/ui and Tailwind, with TanStack Router, Query, Table and Form. Search, AND/OR and filters run in the backend; here a small script stands in for it. You can also mix them: for example Dispatch's feed with Ledger's data table.
